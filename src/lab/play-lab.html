      Football checks

        Zone defenders drop to their landmark, then from 0.6 s read the receivers in their zone: underneath defenders wall or sit under the shallowest one, deep defenders stay deeper than the deepest. A solid orange line shows whom each one is playing; a dotted one is a man matchup.

        Positions come from the movement model: alignment, path, speed profile and time. Nothing is placed by hand. A call that does not fit (a motion with no one to move, a protection with no back, more rushers than blockers and no hot route) is not a legitimate play and is not drawn. Rules are NFHS.
