How a rep works

First the call: see where everyone lines up and what each player does. Then break the huddle: you're at the line. Go through your whole routine on the play clock: check your alignment, read the defense (shell, box, corners, tells), count the rush, change up to two routes, set your key and escape lane, and pick your reads. Drag the field or use Look left / Look right to turn your head. The play stops at the snap: your drop, the coverage, the blitz, and whether to look off the safety. It stops again a second later with your eyes on your first read: throw, roll out, run, or throw it away.

Rooftop Run: you're a courier on the rooftops at night, carrying a package. Each wave of drones comes at you like a pass rush (a rush up the middle, off the edge, a blitz, everybody at once), always from in front where you can see them. When a drone turns red it's locked on: a darting one flies straight down its red line on the roof, so get off the line; a diving one comes down on the red ring on your spot and follows you sideways, so step up under it or drop back. Some waves need two or three moves, like up and then around. Move too early and they follow you; get clear at the last second for a bonus. Pick righty or lefty controls on the start card. Righty: move with the arrow keys and toss with Q W E R T, or on the iPad flick the joystick on the right and toss with the buttons bottom left. Lefty is flipped: W A S D and Y U I O P, the joystick on the left and the buttons bottom right. One key press is one step; on the iPad put a finger down anywhere on the joystick side and flick: one flick is one step, and a sharp flick moves further and faster. Your friends stand on the rooftops around you, numbered 1, 2, 3… from left to right. When one raises a gold lantern, toss him the package with his number (his key, the number keys, or the numbered buttons on the iPad). A catch sends you leaping to the next roof, and catches in a row light up the city. Getting hit ends the run, and so does tossing to a friend whose watcher drone is still on him. A game controller works too (d-pad or a flick of the stick to move).
