How a rep works

First the call: see where everyone lines up and what each player does. Then break the huddle: you're at the line. Before the clock runs out, go through your routine: check your alignment, read the defense (shell, box, corners, tells), count the rush, change up to two routes, and set your key, drop and escape lane. Drag the field or use Look left / Look right to turn your head. After the snap the play runs and freezes 1 second in: name the coverage and decide: throw, run, or throw it away.
